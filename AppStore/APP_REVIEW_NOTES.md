## Notes for the reviewer (paste into "Notes", English, max 4,000 characters)

```
Thank you for reviewing The Last Rail.

OVERVIEW
The Last Rail is an offline, single-player exploration and train-building game for iPhone, played in landscape. All in-game text is in Swedish. There are no accounts, no network features, no ads, no in-app purchases and no data collection.

HOW TO PLAY (first 10–15 minutes)
- Tap "Nytt spel" (New Game). A 10-step guided tutorial starts.
- Move: drag your thumb anywhere on the left half of the screen (floating joystick).
- Sprint: hold the runner button (bottom right).
- Interact: tap the hand button (bottom right). It glows when something is in reach, and a label above it says what will happen.
- Top-right buttons: Backpack (Ryggsäck), Map (Karta), Journal, Pause.
- Tutorial: collect 6 scrap iron, find fuel in the shed north of the locomotive shed, repair the locomotive, open the map, travel to Tallkorset, explore it, then build the workshop (Bygg > Grundläggande verkstad).
- The tutorial can be skipped at any time: Pause > "Hoppa över tutorial".

GAME CONTENT
After the tutorial the player travels between five stations, gathers resources, crafts upgrades and wagons, and finds five optional secrets. The route north to "???" (Polaris) is intentionally blocked by a rockslide. It is the story's cliffhanger at the end of Chapter 1 and is not a broken feature. When health reaches zero the player faints and wakes up by the train, losing half of the resources carried in the backpack. There is no combat.

SAVING
The game saves automatically, including when the app goes to the background. "Fortsätt" (Continue) on the main menu resumes the last save.

PRIVACY
Save files are stored locally in the app's Application Support folder. The app makes no network requests and contains no third-party SDKs.

Contact: [OWNER NAME], [OWNER EMAIL]
```
