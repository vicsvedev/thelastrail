# Privacy Policy – The Last Rail

Published at: https://vicsvedev.github.io/thelastrail/ (source: `docs/index.html`)

Effective 13 October 2026.

The Last Rail is an offline, single-player game. It does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

* The game does not connect to the internet.
* No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
* No accounts and no third-party services.
* The game requests no access to the camera, location, contacts, photos or any other permission.

Your saved game and settings are stored only on your device and contain no personal data. iOS may include them in your own backup (iCloud or computer).

Deleting the app also deletes the save files.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
