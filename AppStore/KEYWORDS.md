# Keywords

Limit: 100 characters per localization, comma-separated, no spaces after commas. Both strings below also stay under 100 **bytes**, so Swedish characters (å, ä, ö) cannot push them over the limit.

The words in the app name (`The Last Rail`) are already indexed and are not repeated here.

## Swedish (primary)

```
tåg,järnväg,lok,pixel,utforska,äventyr,bygga,resurser,mysterium,offline,retro,räls,station
```
90 characters, 95 bytes.

## English (U.K./U.S. store localization)

```
train,railway,locomotive,pixel,explore,adventure,crafting,station,mystery,offline,retro,abandoned
```
97 characters, 97 bytes.

## Guidelines followed

* No competitor names, trademarks or other games' titles (App Review Guideline 2.3.7).
* No words describing features the game lacks: no "multiplayer", "survival", "RPG", "free" or "simulator".
* No category names. The category is set separately, and words like "game" are of little use.
