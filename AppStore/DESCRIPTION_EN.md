# Description – English (store localization)

Paste the text between the rules into the **Description** field in App Store Connect (max 4,000 characters). It contains no Markdown, because the App Store does not render it.

The game itself is in Swedish only. The description says so, so that English-speaking customers are not misled.

---

You wake up in an abandoned locomotive shed. The rail network is silent, the stations are empty, and through the static of an old radio a voice repeats: "If anyone can still hear this... follow the tracks north."

The Last Rail is a calm, atmospheric pixel-art exploration game. Travel by old diesel locomotive between abandoned stations, gather resources, build up your train and slowly piece together what happened to the people who left everything behind.

EXPLORE AN ABANDONED WORLD
Walk through five stations, each with its own setting: a depot on the plains, a forest junction in the rain, a rusting industrial town, a mining village with pitch-black tunnels and a mountain station high in the pass. Watch out for sludge, falling rocks and scalding steam vents.

TRAVEL BY LOCOMOTIVE
Repair Locomotive 7 and head out onto the tracks. Plan your journeys on the railway map: every route costs fuel, and a heavy train runs slower and burns more.

BUILD AND UPGRADE YOUR TRAIN
Build a workshop in your cargo wagon and craft tools, upgrades and new wagons: a workshop wagon, a living wagon and extra cargo wagons. Improve the engine, fuel efficiency and storage, and repair collapsed track to open new routes.

COLLECT RESOURCES
Search for scrap iron, wood, copper, coal, fuel, machine parts, food and research parts. Your backpack only holds so much, so choose what to carry back to the train.

DISCOVER HIDDEN PLACES
Every station rewards careful explorers: hidden caches, a locked door with a code to crack and a walled-up passage deep inside the mine.

UNCOVER THE MYSTERY
Read the diaries, notes and radio logs you find along the way. What was Project Winterlight? Why was the Northern Line closed? And who is still broadcasting from the other side of the mountain?

MADE FOR IPHONE
- Played in landscape with touch controls: joystick, action button and sprint button
- A step-by-step guide at the start, which you can skip
- Saves automatically, so you can continue where you left off
- Works entirely offline
- No ads, no in-app purchases and no data collection

Version 1.0 contains the first chapter of the story.

Language: the game's text is in Swedish.
